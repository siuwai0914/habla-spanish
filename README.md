Habla Spanish 🇪🇸

Welcome to your personal Habla Spanish hub! This is a fun, interactive audio and vocabulary studio designed to help you practice and master everything you are learning in your Spanish course—all in one place.

What You Can Do Here:

🔢 Los Números (1–30): Tap any number card to hear crystal-clear native Spanish pronunciation and phonetic guides. Practice them all or filter by tens.

🔤 El Abecedario (The Alphabet): Master the 27 Spanish letter names (efe, hache, eñe, zeta...) and example words. Use the "Letters Only" button to practice the alphabet song rhythm, or "Letters + Examples" to hear the vocabulary words!

💼 Las Profesiones (Jobs): Learn both masculine and feminine job titles (el dependiente / la dependienta) with dedicated audio triggers.

⏰ Rutina Diaria (Daily Routines): Practice everyday verbs (desayunar, comer, cenar, vivir) paired with real context sentences and time expressions (por la mañana, a mediodía, los fines de semana).

🏙️ El Barrio y la Ciudad (Neighborhood & City): Explore textbook vocabulary for city services (estación de metro, biblioteca, ayuntamiento, comisaría), opposites and adjectives (ruidoso/tranquilo, estrecho/ancho), and locations and directions (norte, sur, este, oeste, centro).

📖 Read Any Text: Paste any Spanish paragraph or textbook exercise into the custom text reader, and the app will read it out loud sentence-by-sentence at your preferred speed!

🎧 Listening Quiz ("Listen & Choose"): Test your ear with random listening challenges, track your score, keep your fire streak (🔥) alive, and use the "Replay super slowly (0.5x)" button whenever you need extra help.

How to Use It:

Just open the website in any web browser, pick a tab at the top, adjust your playback speed (Super Slow, Slow, or Normal), and start listening! No downloads or installations needed.
